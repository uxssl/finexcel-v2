Who we are

Built by practitioners, for the institutions they know. Advisory, training and dialogue for Bangladesh's banks and NBFIs.

FinExcel — Financial Excellence Limited — was founded by financial-sector experts and institutions to train, advise and convene Bangladesh's banks and NBFIs. Our programmes are designed and taught by senior bankers, our advice is grounded in evidence, and our roundtables bring leaders, regulators and government bodies to the same table.
