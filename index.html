Who we are

Built by practitioners, for the institutions they know. Advisory, training and dialogue for Bangladesh's banks and NBFIs.

FinExcel — Financial Excellence Limited — was founded by financial-sector experts and institutions to train, advise and convene Bangladesh's banks and NBFIs. Our programmes are designed and taught by senior bankers, our advice is grounded in evidence, and our roundtables bring leaders, regulators and government bodies to the same table.

More about FinExcel More about FinExcel

To train, advise and convene Bangladesh's banks and NBFIs — so decisions are grounded in evidence and teams are equipped to carry them out.

Programmes are designed and taught by senior bankers and specialists, with cases, regulation and market realities drawn from Bangladesh's own institutions.

Banks, NBFIs, corporates, development organisations, industry bodies and financial professionals — and international firms looking at the Bangladesh market.
